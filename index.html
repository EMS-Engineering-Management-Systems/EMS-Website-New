Inside a Distribution Panel

Hover or tap each numbered component to explore what's inside

1
Component 01
Main Incomer MCCB
Moulded Case Circuit Breaker — the main switch protecting the entire panel from overload and short-circuit faults.
125A / 4-Pole / 6kA
2
Component 02
3-Phase Busbar System
Copper busbars (L1/L2/L3) distribute power from the main incomer to all outgoing circuits. Colour coded: Red, Yellow, Blue.
400V AC / 3Φ + N
3
Component 03
RCCB / RCD Unit
Residual Current Circuit Breaker trips within 30ms on earth leakage above 30mA, providing life safety protection.
63A / 30mA / IΔn Type A
4
Component 04
MCB Row — Power Circuits
Miniature Circuit Breakers for AC & socket-outlet circuits. Rated 20–40A, each protects one outgoing circuit from overload.
20–40A / 6kA / Curve C
5
Component 05
MCB Row — Lighting & Smalls
Smaller MCBs for lighting circuits, low-power equipment, and a combined RCCB for bathroom/wet-area socket circuits.
16–32A / 4.5kA / Resi9
6
Component 06
Neutral Bar (N)
Insulated copper bar consolidating neutral returns from all outgoing circuits back to the incoming supply neutral.
Tinned Copper / Insulated
7
Component 07
Earth Bar (PE)
Protective Earth bar bonded directly to the metalwork. All circuit earth conductors and the panel enclosure connect here.
Green/Yellow / TN-S System
8
Component 08
Surge Protection Device
SPD clamps transient voltage spikes from lightning or switching surges, protecting all downstream equipment.
Type 2 / Up ≤ 1.5kV
9
Component 09
Cable Trunking / Duct
Vertical PVC or steel trunking manages and conceals wiring between components, ensuring a neat and safe installation.
DIN 60mm / Slotted
10
Component 10
Cable Entry Gland Plate
Bottom gland plate with knock-outs for cable entry. Fitted with IP-rated glands to maintain enclosure protection rating.
IP30–IP65 / M20–M50 Glands

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EMS and our services

EMS provides full-scope MEP (Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing) contracting and automation services including: LV/MV electrical distribution, HVAC & chilled water systems, fire alarm & suppression, CCTV & access control, BMS, PLC/SCADA automation, solar PV systems, and our proprietary ZETA smart building platform. We cover design, supply, installation, commissioning, and maintenance.

Yes. EMS was founded in the UAE in 2012 and expanded to Egypt in 2016. We actively work across the GCC region and North Africa, and have completed projects in Saudi Arabia and Kuwait. For international inquiries, contact our UAE office directly at +971563750916.

ZETA is EMS's proprietary AI-powered smart automation platform that integrates BMS, IoT sensors, SCADA, and energy management into a single dashboard. It is available as a SaaS subscription or as a licensed on-premise deployment. Pricing is project-specific — contact us for a scoping call and we will provide a tailored proposal within 5 business days.

The quickest way is to fill out our contact form below with your project type, location, and approximate scope. Alternatively, call or WhatsApp our nearest office directly. For product quotes, use the "Add to Quote" feature on our Products page to build a list, then click "Send Quote Request" — it will pre-fill the contact form with your selected items.

All distribution boards and switchgear we supply and manufacture comply with IEC 61439 (LV switchgear assemblies). Our panels are tested to Form 1–4b separation depending on application. We supply OEM products from Schneider Electric, Siemens, and ABB — all carrying full factory certifications — as well as custom-engineered panels built to your project specification.

Timelines vary by scope. A standard apartment electrical fit-out takes 2–4 weeks. A commercial BMS installation runs 4–12 weeks. Large industrial MEP packages (SCADA + MV/LV + HVAC) typically span 3–12 months. We use Primavera P6 for detailed project scheduling and provide a full programme with every proposal.

Yes. We offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) covering preventive maintenance, emergency callout, spare parts supply, and remote ZETA Platform support. Our support team is available 24/7 for critical systems. AMC pricing starts from a fixed percentage of the installation value, discussed at project handover.
